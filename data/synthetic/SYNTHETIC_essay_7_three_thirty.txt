NOTE: SYNTHETIC (fictional student). Purpose: a strong story from a student with a lower test score.

My day has two parts, and the second one starts at three thirty. That is when I pick up my brother and sister, walk them home, and start dinner before my mother leaves for her night shift. I have done my homework at the kitchen counter for four years, standing up, between stirring and checking spelling words.

On Saturdays I work at the pharmacy. I started at the register. Now the pharmacist lets me call patients when their refills are late. Most of them are not forgetful. They are choosing between the medicine and something else, and nobody had asked. I learned to ask the question slowly, and then to stop talking.

I do not have a long list of clubs. What I have is four years of being the person who shows up at three thirty. I want to be a nurse because I already know what it is to be responsible for someone when you are tired, and I know that I do not walk away.
