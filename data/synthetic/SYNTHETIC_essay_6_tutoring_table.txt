NOTE: SYNTHETIC (fictional student). Purpose: a warm, specific essay about starting something small.

The tutoring table started because I was tired of eating lunch alone in the library. A ninth grader at the next table was stuck on the same factoring problem for twenty minutes, so I moved my tray over. The next day she brought a friend. By October there were six of us, and I had to learn how to explain one idea three different ways, because the first way only ever worked for me.

I kept a notebook of what did not work. I wrote down every explanation that made someone's face go blank. That notebook taught me more about how people learn than any class has. I found out that being good at something and being good at helping someone else get good at it are two separate skills, and I only had the first one.

At the coffee shop where I work, I train the new hires now. I use the same rule: if they look lost, it is my explanation that is wrong, not them. I want to study psychology because I want to understand why that rule works.
