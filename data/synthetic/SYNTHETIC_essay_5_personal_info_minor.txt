My name is Quentin Marbleby, I am seventeen, and I live at 22 Imaginary Court, Nowhereville, ZZ 00000. If you need to reach me, my phone number is 555-0147 and my school is the fictional Larkspur Valley High School.

I want to tell you about the year I was diagnosed with type 1 diabetes. I was fourteen. My mother cried in the hospital parking lot and told me not to tell anyone at school. For months I hid my glucose monitor in a sock. My mother worked two jobs and we had no insurance, so my father, who is undocumented, drove me to the clinic across the county line because he was afraid of the closer one.

Everything changed on a school trip when my sensor alarm went off in front of the whole bus. A girl I barely knew passed me a juice box without saying anything. I realised hiding was costing me more than the diabetes was.

Since then I have run a peer group for newly diagnosed students. We meet on Thursdays in Room 114. I have taught eleven students to count carbohydrates, and I have learned to say my diagnosis out loud. I would like to study nutrition science so that no kid has to learn it alone in a parking lot.
