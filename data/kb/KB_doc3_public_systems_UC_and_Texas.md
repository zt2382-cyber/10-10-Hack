# Two public systems that publish more than one number per college: University of California (fall 2026) and Texas public universities (fall 2025)

Sources: UC campus pages https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/campuses-majors/first-year-admit-data.html (fetched 2026-10-10; fall 2026 admitted-class snapshot); Texas Higher Education Coordinating Board workbook https://reportcenter.highered.texas.gov/reports/data/applicants-acceptance-and-enrollment-2017-2025 (fetched 2026-10-10; Fall 2025 sheet); UT Austin policy page https://admissions.utexas.edu/apply/review-decision-process (fetched 2026-10-10).

## University of California: first-year admit data, fall 2026

UC's page says GPA is the student's grade point average in the A-G subjects, computed from 10th and 11th grade coursework including up to eight honors courses. The page gives applicants, admits, the admit rate, and the middle 25%-75% of ADMITTED students' GPA. It does not give admit rates by GPA band.

- UC Berkeley: 133,154 applicants, 13,967 admits, admit rate 10.5%; middle 50% of admitted students had a GPA of 4.16 to 4.28. Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/campuses-majors/berkeley/first-year-admit-data.html (fall 2026).
- UC Davis: 104,864 applicants, 48,015 admits, admit rate 45.8%; middle 50% of admitted students had a GPA of 4.00 to 4.25. Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/campuses-majors/davis/first-year-admit-data.html (fall 2026).
- UC Irvine: 126,005 applicants, 38,165 admits, admit rate 30.3%; middle 50% of admitted students had a GPA of 4.06 to 4.27. Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/campuses-majors/irvine/first-year-admit-data.html (fall 2026).
- UCLA: 146,692 applicants, 15,903 admits, admit rate 10.8%; middle 50% of admitted students had a GPA of 4.20 to 4.30. Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/campuses-majors/ucla/first-year-admit-data.html (fall 2026).
- UC Merced: 49,426 applicants, 46,812 admits, admit rate 94.7%; middle 50% of admitted students had a GPA of 3.57 to 4.16. Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/campuses-majors/merced/first-year-admit-data.html (fall 2026).
- UC Riverside: 72,542 applicants, 63,958 admits, admit rate 88.2%; middle 50% of admitted students had a GPA of 3.66 to 4.17. Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/campuses-majors/riverside/first-year-admit-data.html (fall 2026).
- UC San Diego: 141,767 applicants, 38,571 admits, admit rate 27.2%; middle 50% of admitted students had a GPA of 4.12 to 4.28. Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/campuses-majors/san-diego/first-year-admit-data.html (fall 2026).
- UC Santa Barbara: 108,512 applicants, 47,716 admits, admit rate 44.0%; middle 50% of admitted students had a GPA of 4.05 to 4.27. Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/campuses-majors/santa-barbara/first-year-admit-data.html (fall 2026).
- UC Santa Cruz: 79,048 applicants, 64,867 admits, admit rate 82.1%; middle 50% of admitted students had a GPA of 3.79 to 4.20. Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/campuses-majors/santa-cruz/first-year-admit-data.html (fall 2026).

## Texas public universities: first-time undergraduate applicants, fall 2025

The Texas workbook reports, for Texas applicants, how many applied, how many were accepted, and how many of those accepted were in the top 10% and in the top 11-25% of their high school class. It does not report how many applied from each rank group, so no admit rate by class rank can be computed from it.

- The University of Texas at Austin: 37,691 Texas applicants, 15,931 accepted; 12,331 of those accepted were in the top 10% of their class and 735 in the top 11-25%. Source: https://reportcenter.highered.texas.gov/reports/data/applicants-acceptance-and-enrollment-2017-2025 (Fall 2025).
- Texas A&M University: 47,537 Texas applicants, 25,052 accepted; 15,611 of those accepted were in the top 10% of their class and 2,502 in the top 11-25%. Source: https://reportcenter.highered.texas.gov/reports/data/applicants-acceptance-and-enrollment-2017-2025 (Fall 2025).
- The University of Texas at Dallas: 28,762 Texas applicants, 20,491 accepted; 5,515 of those accepted were in the top 10% of their class and 4,075 in the top 11-25%. Source: https://reportcenter.highered.texas.gov/reports/data/applicants-acceptance-and-enrollment-2017-2025 (Fall 2025).
- Texas Tech University: 25,474 Texas applicants, 22,074 accepted; 6,196 of those accepted were in the top 10% of their class and 6,839 in the top 11-25%. Source: https://reportcenter.highered.texas.gov/reports/data/applicants-acceptance-and-enrollment-2017-2025 (Fall 2025).
- University of Houston: 29,329 Texas applicants, 22,739 accepted; 5,701 of those accepted were in the top 10% of their class and 5,842 in the top 11-25%. Source: https://reportcenter.highered.texas.gov/reports/data/applicants-acceptance-and-enrollment-2017-2025 (Fall 2025).

## UT Austin automatic admission (policy text, page fetched 2026-10-10)

The UT Austin review-and-decision page lists the automatic-admission threshold as Top 6% for Summer/Fall 2025 and Spring 2026 applicants, and Top 5% for Summer/Fall 2026 and Spring 2027 applicants. Source: https://admissions.utexas.edu/apply/review-decision-process. The page also says it publishes Automatic Admission Reports (not downloaded for this document).
