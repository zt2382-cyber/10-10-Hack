# What Cornell University says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://irp.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/CDS-Cornell-2025-2026-v2.xlsx  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- In 2025-26, Cornell admitted 6,077 of 72,523 first-year applicants (8.4%).
- Cornell (2025-26) admitted 1,720 of 12,978 in-state, 3,693 of 40,979 out-of-state and 664 of 18,566 international applicants.
- Cornell (2025-26) offered 9,720 students a wait-list place; 6,598 accepted and 254 were later admitted.
- Among Cornell enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (43% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1490-1550 (2025-26).
