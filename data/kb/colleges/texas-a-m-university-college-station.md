# What Texas A&M University-College Station says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://abpa.tamu.edu/getattachment/3ef3380e-0c01-4ac3-bad5-185eb51fa208/CDS-PDF-2025-2026_TAMU_FINAL.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- Texas A&M rates the application essay as Important (the second of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Texas A&M rates standardized test scores as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Texas A&M rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Texas A&M rates class rank as Very Important (the top of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- In 2025-26, Texas A&M admitted 32,531 of 62,967 first-year applicants (51.7%).
- Texas A&M (2025-26) admitted 26,620 of 49,156 in-state, 4,489 of 10,882 out-of-state and 1,422 of 2,929 international applicants.
- Among Texas A&M enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (76.72% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1160-1390 (2025-26).
