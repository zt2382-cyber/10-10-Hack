# What Boston University says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://www.bu.edu/asir/files/2026/07/CDS-2025-2026-updated.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- Boston University rates the application essay as Important (the second of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Boston University rates standardized test scores as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Boston University rates demonstrated interest in the college as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Boston University rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Boston University rates class rank as Important (the second of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- In 2025-26, Boston University admitted 9,853 of 76,776 first-year applicants (12.8%).
- Among Boston University enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (36% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1420-1510 (2025-26).
