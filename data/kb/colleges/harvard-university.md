# What Harvard University says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.harvard.edu/dist/6/210/files/2026/07/CDS_2025-2026.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- Harvard rates the application essay as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Harvard rates standardized test scores as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Harvard rates demonstrated interest in the college as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Harvard rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Harvard rates class rank as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- In 2025-26, Harvard admitted 2,003 of 47,893 first-year applicants (4.2%).
