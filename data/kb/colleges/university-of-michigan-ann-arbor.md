# What University of Michigan-Ann Arbor says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://obp.umich.edu/wp-content/uploads/pubdata/cds/cds_2025-26_umaa.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- In 2025-26, University of Michigan admitted 17,915 of 109,112 first-year applicants (16.4%).
- University of Michigan (2025-26) offered 24,447 students a wait-list place; 18,435 accepted and 1,663 were later admitted.
- Among University of Michigan enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (55% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1370-1530 (2025-26).
