# What University of California-Los Angeles says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://apb.ucla.edu/file/d1ab04b3-ee89-4cf2-9124-8dc0471b5e5a  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- UCLA rates the application essay as Very Important (the top of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- UCLA rates standardized test scores as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- UCLA rates demonstrated interest in the college as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- UCLA rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- UCLA rates class rank as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- In 2025-26, UCLA admitted 13,659 of 145,086 first-year applicants (9.4%).
- UCLA (2025-26) offered 20,563 students a wait-list place; 13,612 accepted and 1,514 were later admitted.
