# What University of Virginia-Main Campus says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://ira.virginia.edu/sites/ira/files/2025-03/CDS_2024-2025_508.pdf  |  Year: 2024-25
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- University of Virginia rates the application essay as Important (the second of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- University of Virginia rates standardized test scores as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- University of Virginia rates demonstrated interest in the college as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- University of Virginia rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- University of Virginia rates class rank as Very Important (the top of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- In 2024-25, University of Virginia admitted 9,909 of 58,951 first-year applicants (16.8%).
- University of Virginia (2024-25) admitted 4,269 of 16,464 in-state, 4,912 of 35,526 out-of-state and 728 of 6,961 international applicants.
- University of Virginia (2024-25) offered 10,470 students a wait-list place; 6,759 accepted and 242 were later admitted.
- Among University of Virginia enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (6% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1410-1520 (2024-25).
