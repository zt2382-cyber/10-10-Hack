# What Tufts University says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://provost.tufts.edu/institutionalresearch/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/CDS_2025-2026.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- In 2025-26, Tufts admitted 3,613 of 33,415 first-year applicants (10.8%).
- Tufts (2025-26) admitted 659 of 4,733 in-state, 2,422 of 19,414 out-of-state and 531 of 9,260 international applicants.
- Tufts (2025-26) offered 3,061 students a wait-list place; 1,193 accepted and 71 were later admitted.
- Among Tufts enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (42% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1460-1520 (2025-26).
