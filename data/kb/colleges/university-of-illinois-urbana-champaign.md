# What University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://www.dmi.illinois.edu/stuenr/misc/cds_2024_2025.xlsx  |  Year: 2024-25
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign rates the application essay as Important (the second of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign rates standardized test scores as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign rates demonstrated interest in the college as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign rates class rank as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- In 2024-25, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign admitted 31,247 of 73,742 first-year applicants (42.4%).
- University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (2024-25) admitted 15,083 of 27,730 in-state, 9,983 of 27,575 out-of-state and 6,181 of 18,437 international applicants.
- Among University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (41.28% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1390-1520 (2024-25).
