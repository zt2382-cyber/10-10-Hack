# What Stanford University says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://drive.usercontent.google.com/download?id=1GIPKgVj1d86dkmLkHI_mZVCk_iY6kiCp&export=download  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- Stanford rates the application essay as Very Important (the top of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Stanford rates standardized test scores as Very Important (the top of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Stanford rates demonstrated interest in the college as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Stanford rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Stanford rates class rank as Very Important (the top of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- In 2025-26, Stanford admitted 2,302 of 60,646 first-year applicants (3.8%).
- Among Stanford enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (55.57% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1520-1570 (2025-26).
