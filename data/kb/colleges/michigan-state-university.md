# What Michigan State University says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://edge.sitecorecloud.io/michiganstab57e-msustrategi129d-prod9868-7e5e/media/project/msu/ir/docs/cds/cds-2025-2026.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- Michigan State University rates the application essay as Important (the second of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Michigan State University rates standardized test scores as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Michigan State University rates demonstrated interest in the college as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Michigan State University rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Michigan State University rates class rank as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- In 2025-26, Michigan State University admitted 52,411 of 62,965 first-year applicants (83.2%).
- Michigan State University (2025-26) admitted 21,067 of 25,102 in-state, 25,087 of 30,768 out-of-state and 6,257 of 7,095 international applicants.
- Among Michigan State University enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (51.5% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1100-1310 (2025-26).
