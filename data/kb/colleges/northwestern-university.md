# What Northwestern University says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://drive.usercontent.google.com/download?id=1w2QtvSXBK8cuV3r10E4g3-beRqP0lilk&export=download  |  Year: 2024-25
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- In 2024-25, Northwestern admitted 3,806 of 49,474 first-year applicants (7.7%).
- Among Northwestern enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (46% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1510-1560 (2024-25).
