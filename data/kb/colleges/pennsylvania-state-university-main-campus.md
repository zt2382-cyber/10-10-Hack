# What Pennsylvania State University-Main Campus says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.psu.edu/dist/d/114442/files/2026/09/CDS_2025_2026_UniversityPark_v3.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- In 2025-26, Penn State admitted 58,952 of 106,547 first-year applicants (55.3%).
- Penn State (2025-26) admitted 12,096 of 21,321 in-state, 38,846 of 72,861 out-of-state and 8,010 of 12,365 international applicants.
- Penn State (2025-26) offered 2,109 students a wait-list place; 1,153 accepted and 46 were later admitted.
- Among Penn State enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (28.6% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1280-1410 (2025-26).
