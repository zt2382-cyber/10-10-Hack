# What Duke University says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://ir.provost.duke.edu/sites/default/files/CDS_PDF_2025-2026-20260622-website-published-version.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- In 2025-26, Duke admitted 2,910 of 55,541 first-year applicants (5.2%).
- Among Duke enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (48% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1510-1570 (2025-26).
