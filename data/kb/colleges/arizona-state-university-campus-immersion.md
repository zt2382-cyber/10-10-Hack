# What Arizona State University Campus Immersion says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://uoia.asu.edu/sites/g/files/litvpz1436/files/2026-04/CDS%202025-26%20-%20ASU%20Campus%20Immersion.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- Arizona State University rates the application essay as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Arizona State University rates standardized test scores as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Arizona State University rates demonstrated interest in the college as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Arizona State University rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Arizona State University rates class rank as Important (the second of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- In 2025-26, Arizona State University admitted 61,533 of 69,617 first-year applicants (88.4%).
- Arizona State University (2025-26) admitted 18,291 of 20,323 in-state, 35,233 of 40,388 out-of-state and 8,009 of 8,906 international applicants.
