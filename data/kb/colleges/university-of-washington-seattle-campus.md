# What University of Washington-Seattle Campus says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://cdn.uw.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/162/2026/02/27111247/CDS_2025-2026_Seattle.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- University of Washington rates the application essay as Very Important (the top of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- University of Washington rates standardized test scores as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- University of Washington rates demonstrated interest in the college as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- University of Washington rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- University of Washington rates class rank as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- In 2025-26, University of Washington admitted 30,446 of 72,933 first-year applicants (41.7%).
- University of Washington (2025-26) admitted 6,980 of 15,260 in-state, 18,813 of 44,619 out-of-state and 4,653 of 13,054 international applicants.
- University of Washington (2025-26) offered 15,363 students a wait-list place; 8,350 accepted and 2,252 were later admitted.
- Among University of Washington enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (19.12% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1320-1502 (2025-26).
