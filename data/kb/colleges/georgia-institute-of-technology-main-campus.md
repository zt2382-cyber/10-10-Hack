# What Georgia Institute of Technology-Main Campus says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://irp.gatech.edu/sites/default/files/CDS/CDS_2025-2026_FINAL_R4_03JUN2026.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- Georgia Tech rates the application essay as Important (the second of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Georgia Tech rates standardized test scores as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Georgia Tech rates demonstrated interest in the college as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Georgia Tech rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Georgia Tech rates class rank as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- In 2025-26, Georgia Tech admitted 8,921 of 66,881 first-year applicants (13.3%).
- Georgia Tech (2025-26) admitted 3,691 of 12,527 in-state, 4,513 of 44,596 out-of-state and 716 of 9,758 international applicants.
- Georgia Tech (2025-26) offered 7,651 students a wait-list place; 4,875 accepted and 653 were later admitted.
- Among Georgia Tech enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (78% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1370-1530 (2025-26).
