# What University of Arizona says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://uair.arizona.edu/sites/default/files/2026-06/CDS_PDF_2025-26_v02_RE-PRINT.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- In 2025-26, University of Arizona admitted 47,080 of 56,376 first-year applicants (83.5%).
- University of Arizona (2025-26) admitted 13,461 of 15,165 in-state, 29,119 of 32,195 out-of-state and 4,500 of 9,016 international applicants.
- Among University of Arizona enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (12% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1090-1320 (2025-26).
