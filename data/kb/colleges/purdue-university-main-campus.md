# What Purdue University-Main Campus says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://www.purdue.edu/idata/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CDS-2025-2026.xlsx  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- In 2025-26, Purdue admitted 37,881 of 87,220 first-year applicants (43.4%).
- Purdue (2025-26) admitted 8,559 of 12,040 in-state, 25,650 of 58,853 out-of-state and 3,672 of 16,327 international applicants.
- Among Purdue enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (83.84% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1220-1470 (2025-26).
