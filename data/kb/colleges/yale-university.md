# What Yale University says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://oir.yale.edu/sites/default/files/yale_cds_2025-26_md_20260616.pdf  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- Yale rates the application essay as Very Important (the top of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Yale rates standardized test scores as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Yale rates demonstrated interest in the college as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Yale rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- Yale rates class rank as Very Important (the top of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2025-26.
- In 2025-26, Yale admitted 2,387 of 50,264 first-year applicants (4.7%).
- Yale (2025-26) offered 944 students a wait-list place; 738 accepted and 32 were later admitted.
- Among Yale enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (67% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1480-1560 (2025-26).
