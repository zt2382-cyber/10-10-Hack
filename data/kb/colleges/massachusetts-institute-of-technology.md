# What Massachusetts Institute of Technology says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://ir.mit.edu/projects/2024-25-common-data-set/  |  Year: 2024-25
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- MIT rates the application essay as Important (the second of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- MIT rates standardized test scores as Important (the second of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- MIT rates demonstrated interest in the college as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- MIT rates a family link to an alumnus or alumna as Not Considered (the lowest of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- MIT rates class rank as Considered (the third of the form's four levels) in its own Common Data Set, section C7, 2024-25.
- MIT (2024-25) offered 590 students a wait-list place; 509 accepted and 9 were later admitted.
- Among MIT enrolled first-year students who submitted SAT scores (83% of them did), the middle 50% scored 1520-1570 (2024-25).
