# What Virginia Polytechnic Institute and State University says in its own Common Data Set

Source: https://aie.vt.edu/content/dam/aie_vt_edu/common-data-set/25-26/2025-2026-CDS.xlsx  |  Year: 2025-26
These are the college's own published statements. They say what the college reports, not how much any factor changes a decision.

- In 2025-26, Virginia Tech admitted 31,515 of 57,755 first-year applicants (54.6%).
- Virginia Tech (2025-26) admitted 9,893 of 20,900 in-state, 18,311 of 31,104 out-of-state and 3,311 of 5,751 international applicants.
- Virginia Tech (2025-26) offered 22,526 students a wait-list place; 13,380 accepted and 4,295 were later admitted.
